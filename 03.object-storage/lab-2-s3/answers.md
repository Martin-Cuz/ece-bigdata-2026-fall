## Kubernetes Job

See `run-job.sh`: the datasets go into a ConfigMap, the credentials into a Secret created from the exported profile
(never printed), and the Job runs `amazon/aws-cli` to upload `users.csv` and `orders.csv` to `bronze/`.

- Why are the credentials stored in a Secret and not in the ConfigMap? A Secret is a distinct Kubernetes resource with
  its own RBAC rules, it is not shown in clear in `kubectl describe`, it can be encrypted at rest and mounted in memory
  (tmpfs). A ConfigMap is meant for public configuration and is readable by anyone allowed to read configuration.
  Both are only base64-encoded by default, so a Secret is not a vault by itself.
- The credentials of Onyxia are temporary. What happens if the Job is executed again tomorrow? The session token has
  expired and the Job fails with `ExpiredToken`. A production platform gives the Job a short-lived identity at
  runtime: an IAM role bound to the Kubernetes service account (IRSA / workload identity), a Vault dynamic secret, or
  an external secrets operator which refreshes the Secret.
- How would you turn this Job into a daily ingestion? Replace the `Job` by a `CronJob` (`schedule: "0 2 * * *"`) that
  reads its data from the source system instead of a ConfigMap, writes into a dated prefix
  (`bronze/orders/date=YYYY-MM-DD/`) to stay idempotent, with `concurrencyPolicy: Forbid` and alerts on failure.
